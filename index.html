Be here now — just this breath
Your mind is moving fast. This practice gently returns the wheel to you — not by fighting thoughts, but by coming home to this breath.
"The quieter you become, the more you can hear."

What this means
When your mind slows down and stops chattering, you become more aware of what's actually happening — inside and around you.
The practice
Sit where you are. Take one slow breath — feel it completely. When a thought arises, say "thinking" and return. Each return is a small homecoming. Stay for 3 minutes.
